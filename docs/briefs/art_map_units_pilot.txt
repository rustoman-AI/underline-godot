ART: MAP UNITS PILOT, ONE HANDCAR (ChatGPT image tool)

WHY
The network map shows handcars carrying goods between stations along the tunnel lines. Today they are drawn by
code and look like placeholders; the owner rejected them. This pilot does what the resident pilot did for the
station: one design, then one animation loop, to see whether the image tool can keep a small machine and its crew
the same from frame to frame. If yes, the same brief grows to the trader car, the squad and the expedition
(docs/briefs/art_map_units_set.txt). One evening, nothing installed.

OUR STYLE (match it exactly)
One consistent dark ink outline, flat colour with a little texture, muted palette, warm lamp light, no gloss, no
3D render look, no photo realism. Attach as style references: incoming-art/anim_pilot/walk_body03_accepted_sheet.png
(the accepted walking resident) and assets/hubs/brickworks.webp (a station banner).

STEP A: THE REFERENCE (one still image)
A railway handcar from the subway tunnels, after the collapse, with two crew. TRUE SIDE VIEW, facing right.
- The car: a low flat deck of scarred planks on a riveted iron frame, two small iron rail wheels with four spokes
  each, a short iron post in the middle carrying a see-saw pump lever (a long wooden beam with a handle bar at each
  end). Two sacks and a small crate lashed with rope at the back. A cloth band hangs along the side of the deck:
  paint it PLAIN LIGHT GREY #C8C8C8, nothing else in the picture uses that grey (the game recolours it per faction).
  A small oil lamp hangs at the front.
- The crew: two subway workers standing on the deck facing each other across the lever, one at each end, both
  hands on their handle. The front one is a slim older man in a dark knitted beanie and navy quilted coat (like
  incoming-art/anim_pilot/reference_body_03.png); the back one is a woman with a dark ponytail and a rust work
  jacket (like incoming-art/anim_pilot/reference_body_01.png). Same clothes as those references.
- Lever level, both crew upright. Wheels sit on a thin pair of rails drawn as one straight line.
- Canvas about 1024 x 512, transparent background (real alpha, no white, no checkerboard, no glow, no halo), the
  car centred with a lot of empty space around it. No ground, no shadow, no text.
Delivery: incoming-art/map_units/handcar_reference_v1.png (v2, v3 if retried) and handcar_reference_prompt.txt.
The owner accepts one reference before Step B.

STEP B: THE PUMP LOOP (one sheet, attach the accepted reference to every attempt)
The same handcar and the same two crew pumping, 6 frames in one row, left to right. Nothing about the car or the
people changes between frames except what moves.
  1. lever level, both crew upright, hands at chest height
  2. front end of the lever going down: the front worker bends forward pushing down, the back worker rises
  3. front end at its lowest: front worker bent deepest, back worker straight with arms up
  4. lever level again, both upright (like frame 1)
  5. back end going down: the back worker bends pushing down, the front worker rises
  6. back end at its lowest: back worker bent deepest, front worker straight with arms up
The wheels turn one sixth of a turn each frame (four spokes, so the loop closes). The sacks, crate, lamp, deck and
cloth band do not move or change. The rails stay one straight line at the same height in every frame.
STRICT LAYOUT
- One row, transparent background, real alpha, no glow, no halo.
- LEAVE A WIDE EMPTY GAP between neighbouring frames, at least a quarter of a frame's width, so no part of one frame
  (lever ends, arms, lamp) comes near the next. This matters more than the exact canvas size.
- Same scale in every frame: the car the same length, the rails at the same height.
- No ground line, no shadow, no motion blur, no speed lines, no numbers, no grid lines, no text.
Delivery: incoming-art/map_units/handcar_pump_v1.png (v2, v3) and handcar_pump_prompt.txt.
If the first sheet drifts (the car changes shape, the crew change clothes, the lever does not alternate), make a
second attempt with the first sheet attached and say "keep frame 1 exactly, redraw the others to match it". Send
what you have after three attempts; do not polish further.

HANDOFF
incoming-art/map_units/HANDOFF.txt: which version you prefer for each step and why, the real canvas size of every
file, frames that touch a gap, and anything that changes between frames that should not.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT (the lead and Codex, not ChatGPT)
The frames are found by the empty gaps (not equal cells), lined up on the rail line, cut into
assets/map/units/handcar_pump/ with tools/cut_map_units.gd, recoloured per faction and played on the map at about
8 frames a second while the car rolls. The owner accepts or rejects on a short clip of the map.
