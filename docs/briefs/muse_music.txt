STEP 2: STYLE (what the music is for)
Dark, quiet, human. Think of the feel of This War of Mine and Frostpunk, not of an action game: sparse piano or felt piano, low strings, a distant brass note, tape hiss, a slow pulse; long notes, silence between phrases. NO heroic melody, NO drums that drive, NO choir, NO vocals or words, NO electronic arpeggios, nothing that sounds like a known song. It must sit under the voice-over and under the sound effects: instrumental, narrow dynamic range, no sudden loud entrances.
