DIG (6 frames): digging into a rock face just outside the frame on the right, with a pickaxe. Side view facing
right, feet apart and planted, they do not move. One loop of a heavy swing:
  1 pick raised high behind the head, body leaning back a little
  2 pick coming over the shoulder, body starting forward
  3 strike: pick head forward at chest to waist height, body bent forward, arms extended
  4 follow through: pick head lower, weight on the front foot
  5 pulling the pick back out, body straightening
  6 lifting again, pick at shoulder height (leads back into frame 1)
The pickaxe is the same tool in every frame and for every person: a dark wooden handle about as long as the
person's arm, a grey iron double head. Same size and colours in every frame; it never bends or changes length.

BUILD (6 frames): building a wooden frame just outside the frame on the right, with a claw hammer. Side view facing
right, feet planted. Left hand holds a nail or steadies a plank at chest height (the plank is not drawn, the hand is
open against it); right hand swings the hammer:
  1 hammer raised to head height, elbow up
  2 hammer coming down
  3 strike: hammer at chest height in front, at the left hand
  4 small bounce back after the strike
  5 hammer lifting again
  6 hammer near head height (leads back into frame 1)
The hammer is the same tool in every frame and for every person: short wooden handle, dark iron head. It never
changes size.
