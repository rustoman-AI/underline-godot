ART: MAP TOKENS, UNITS AND CARAVANS ON THE NETWORK MAP (ChatGPT image tool)

WHERE WE ARE
The network map now shows what moves between stations: carts carrying loads home along supply lines, traders
walking to stations we have a deal with, squads standing on stations, and the expedition party in its tunnel.
Today all of these are drawn by code as plain shapes (a brown box on two wheels, a paper disc with an icon).
The owner wants them to look like a finished game: small painted tabletop miniatures standing on the paper map,
the way a board game puts carved pieces on its board. This set replaces the code shapes with real art.

LOOK
- Painted tabletop miniatures, seen from about 30 degrees above (three-quarter top-down), side facing RIGHT.
- Ink outline like the map pins and the hub banners, flat colour with one soft shade, no photo realism.
- Underground post-collapse New York: patched canvas, scrap wood, rope, gas masks, lamps. No guns pointed at the
  viewer, no gore, no flags or real emblems, no text, no numbers.
- Every piece stands on the same small round base: dark wood disc, 1 px lighter rim, seen in the same perspective.
- Colour rule: the part that shows who owns the piece is painted PLAIN LIGHT GREY (#C8C8C8) and nothing else in the
  picture uses that grey. The game tints that part in the owner's colour (the Depot red, the Exchange green and so on).
  Mark it on: the cloth band on a cart, the canopy of the trader cart, the armbands or scarves of the squad, the
  pennant on the expedition pole.
Attach as reference: assets/map/pins.webp (the station pins) and two hub banners from incoming-art/hubs/style_v2.

THE PIECES (one image each, in this order)
1. supply_cart: a two-wheeled hand cart of scrap planks with a grey cloth band (tint part) round the bed, the bed
   EMPTY and open on top (the game draws the load above it), a pull shaft pointing right, a small lamp on a hook.
2. supply_cart_pull: the same cart with one hooded porter in front, leaning into the shaft, mid-step.
3. trader_cart: a bigger four-wheeled cart under a grey canvas canopy (tint part), bundles and a crate showing at the
   back, a hanging lantern, one trader walking beside it.
4. squad: three standing figures shoulder to shoulder on one base, gas masks, padded coats, grey armbands (tint
   part), one holding a lamp up, one with a long pipe-spear resting on the shoulder, one with a scrap shield.
5. squad_small: one figure from piece 4 alone on the base (used when the squad is very small or zoomed far out).
6. expedition: two figures walking right, big packs and coiled rope, the front one holds a pole with a small grey
   pennant (tint part) and a lamp, the back one carries a pick.
7. settlers: one porter carrying a stack of planks and a sack on the back, walking right (building materials going out
   to a newly taken station).

STRICT LAYOUT
- Transparent background, real alpha, no white, no checkerboard, no glow, no halo, no drop shadow (the game draws it).
- Canvas 512 x 512 for each piece, the base centred horizontally, the bottom of the base 40 px above the bottom edge.
- The base is 300 px wide in every image; pieces 1, 2, 5 and 7 fit inside a 360 px wide box, pieces 3, 4 and 6
  inside 440 px. Nothing touches the canvas edge.
- Must still read when the game shows it 40 px tall: big clear shapes, thick outline, few small details.

DELIVERY
incoming-art/map_tokens/<piece>_v1.png (v2, v3 if retried) and <piece>_prompt.txt with the exact prompt, plus
HANDOFF.txt (preferred version per piece, known defects). At most three attempts per piece.
Do not install anything into assets/.

WHAT THE LEAD DOES AFTER
Checks alpha and the tint grey, scales to 64 and 128 px, puts them on the map in place of the code shapes (tinted
by owner, flipped when walking left, the load icon still above the cart), and sends the owner a clip of the map with
carts, traders and squads moving.
