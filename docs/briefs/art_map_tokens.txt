ART: MAP UNITS, ANIMATED (ChatGPT image tool) — version 2, replaces version 1 of 19 Oct

WHERE WE ARE
The network map shows what moves between stations: handcars carrying loads home along supply lines, trader
handcars going to stations we have a deal with, rival handcars going to their seats, squads walking from station
to station, and the expedition party in its tunnel. Today all of them are drawn by code with lines and circles.
The owner calls that a meme and wants finished, painted art that moves in real frames, like the residents in the
station (animation sets 1 and 2, incoming-art/anim_set1, incoming-art/anim_set2). This set gives the map that.

LOOK (same for every sheet)
- Painted miniatures seen from about 30 degrees above (three-quarter top-down), moving to the RIGHT.
- Ink outline and flat colour with one soft shade, the same hand as the station pins (assets/map/pins.webp) and the
  hub banners (assets/hubs/*.webp). No photo realism, no 3D render look, no glow.
- Underground post-collapse New York: scrap wood, riveted iron, patched canvas, rope, gas masks, oil lamps.
  No flags, no real emblems, no text, no numbers, no guns pointed at the viewer, no gore.
- OWNER COLOUR: the part that tells whose unit it is is painted PLAIN LIGHT GREY #C8C8C8 and nothing else in the
  picture uses that grey (the game tints it: Depot rust, Exchange green, Directorate red, Synod blue, Lamplighters
  amber). Tint parts: the cloth band on a handcar, the trader's canopy, the squad's armbands, the expedition pennant.
- Attach as reference for every sheet: assets/map/pins.webp, two banners from assets/hubs (riverside.webp,
  brickworks.webp), and for the people one accepted set 1 sheet (incoming-art/anim_set1/walk_body01_v1.png) so faces
  and clothes match the station.

THE SHEETS (one image each, in this order)
1. handcar_pump: a railway handcar on two iron wheels, a plank deck with a grey cloth band (tint part) along the
   side, a see-saw pump lever on a post in the middle, two crew facing each other pumping it, sacks lashed at the
   back. 8 frames, one full pump cycle: lever level, front end down, front end lowest, back to level, back end
   down, back end lowest, rising, level. Wheels turn a quarter turn per two frames (four spokes, so it loops). The
   crew bend and straighten with their end of the lever. Same handcar size and position in every frame.
2. handcar_idle: the same handcar standing still at a station, crew resting, one wiping the brow, one checking a
   sack. 4 frames, slow breathing loop.
3. trader_car: a bigger handcar with a grey canvas canopy (tint part) over crates and bundles, a hanging lantern
   that swings, one trader pumping a side lever. 8 frames, one cycle, wheels turning as in sheet 1.
4. squad_walk: three figures in step on foot (no base): gas masks, padded coats, grey armbands (tint part), one
   with a lamp held up, one with a long pipe-spear on the shoulder, one with a scrap shield. 8 frames, one full walk
   cycle (two steps), feet planted cleanly, nobody slides.
5. squad_stand: the same three standing guard, small shifts of weight, the lamp flame flickering. 4 frames.
6. expedition_walk: two figures with big packs and coiled rope, the front one with a pole carrying a small grey
   pennant (tint part) and a lamp, the back one with a pick. 8 frames, one walk cycle, heavier and slower than 4.
7. puff: a small grey-brown dust puff kicked up by wheels, 6 frames from appearing to gone. Separate, so the game
   can place it behind any unit.

STRICT LAYOUT
- Transparent background, real alpha, no white, no checkerboard, no glow, no halo, no drop shadow (the game draws
  the shadow).
- One row of equal cells, each 384 wide x 384 tall: 8 frames = 3072 x 384, 4 frames = 1536 x 384, 6 = 2304 x 384.
- The unit sits centred in every cell; the rails or the soles on one line 48 px above the bottom edge in every
  frame. Handcars and the trader car fill about 300 px of width; the squad and expedition about 260 px; nothing
  touches or crosses into the next cell.
- Must still read when shown 48 px tall: big clear shapes, thick outline, few small details.
- No ground line, no grid lines, no motion blur, no speed lines, no text.

DELIVERY
incoming-art/map_units/<sheet name>_v1.png (v2, v3 if retried) and <sheet name>_prompt.txt with the exact prompt,
plus HANDOFF.txt (preferred version per sheet, frame defects, where the tint grey is). At most three attempts per
sheet. Do not install anything into assets/.

WHAT HAPPENS AFTER
Codex (docs/briefs/codex_map_units.txt) cuts the cells, lines up the rails and soles, puts the frames into
assets/map/units/, and plays them on the map tinted by owner, flipped when moving left, at the fps in its brief.
