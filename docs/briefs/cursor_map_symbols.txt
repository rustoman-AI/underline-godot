CURSOR: FACTION SYMBOLS ON THE NETWORK MAP
The map (ui/network_view.gd) is the overseer's desk: painted city map, owner-coloured pin sprites (PIN_SPRITE by owner), paper tags with a coloured strip on the left (_paint_tag), order flags, squad and intent badges. Faction symbols exist (assets/factions/<faction_id>_symbol.png: depot, directorate, synod, exchange, black_flag = The Lamplighters) but the map does not use them. Add them so the player reads whose station each pin is without relying on colour alone. Local only. No push. Do not change the map, station positions, balance, pin layout/collision logic or any gameplay.

1. FACTION OF A STATION. Add one helper (in network_view.gd or game) station -> faction id: use the owner when it is one of depot, directorate, synod, exchange. For neutral/other stations use the community: stations whose community is the Lamplighters (brickworks) map to black_flag; Synod/Exchange/Directorate/Depot-leaning stations that are not owned keep no symbol unless the station data says they belong to that faction (check data/stations.json owner and data/station_communities.json lean; if unclear, show a symbol only for owned stations and for brickworks). Bunkers, ruins, enemies and plain neutrals get no symbol (keep the existing strip colour).
2. TAGS. In _paint_tag, replace the coloured strip with a small symbol badge for stations that have a faction: about 18 to 22 px at the normal zoom, left of the title, widen the tag by the badge width so text does not shrink. Stations without a faction keep the strip. The symbols are drawn in faction colour on cream paper; if one reads poorly, draw it as an ink stamp (single colour, slightly rotated per station like the tag tilt, a little transparency so the paper shows through). Check all five on the paper colour at zoom levels 0, 1 and 2.
3. PINS. Capitals only (capital: true): a small symbol stamped on the pin head or on the paper beside the pin, whichever reads better at pin size. Do not add anything to ordinary pins.
4. STATION SHEET AND VISIT SCREEN. The faction symbol next to the community name in the sheet header and in the visit header (the visit header already has a small station portrait; do not break that layout).
5. LEGEND. A small paper legend on the desk (corner, collapsible or always on, your choice) listing the five symbols with the faction names in EN and RU. Hide it when it would cover pins; do not add any other UI.
6. FALLBACK. If a symbol file is missing, fall back to today's strip.
7. CHECKS. bot_run, check_audience, check_save, check_network, check_slice. Screenshots EN and RU at 1280x720 and 1920x1080: the full map at start of a run, the map after the player owns more stations, a zoomed region around Grand Central, one around Wall St and one around Atlantic Av (Lamplighters), and the sheet/visit headers. Report any tag that became wider than before and overlapped something.
8. COMMIT locally: "Network map: faction symbols on tags, capitals, sheets and legend". Do not commit incoming-art/.
