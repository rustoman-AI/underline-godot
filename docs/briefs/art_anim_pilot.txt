ART: ANIMATION PILOT, ONE WALK CYCLE (ChatGPT image tool)

WHY
The station's people are single still pictures. Moving a still picture around reads as jumping, so the game needs real frames. This pilot tests one thing only: can the image tool draw one of our residents in 8 walk frames that stay the same person from frame to frame. If yes, the same brief grows to standing, working and digging for a handful of body types. If not, we switch to a 3D-to-2D pipeline or an animator. One evening, nothing installed.

REFERENCE
Attach incoming-art/anim_pilot/reference_body_03.png (212x660, transparent). This is the character: a slim older man in a dark knitted beanie, white stubble and moustache, a quilted navy blue coat with a brown patch on the left shoulder, a rust-red shirt under it, grey-brown work trousers with patches and a tear on the knee, brown work boots. Our drawing style: one consistent dark ink outline, flat colour with a little texture, muted palette, no gloss.

WHAT TO DRAW
One image: the same man walking, 8 frames, in one row, left to right.
- True side view, facing right. (The reference is three-quarter; turn him to a clean profile, same clothes.)
- He walks empty-handed, so both arms swing.
- Both boots the same brown in every frame.
- The 8 frames are one loop of a calm, tired walk, in this order:
  1. right heel touches the ground in front, left leg behind (contact)
  2. weight drops onto the right leg, body lowest (down)
  3. left leg passes the right, body upright (passing)
  4. body highest, pushing off the right toe (up)
  5. left heel touches in front, right leg behind (contact, mirror of 1)
  6. weight drops onto the left leg (down)
  7. right leg passes the left (passing)
  8. pushing off the left toe (up)
  Arms swing against the legs: right leg forward, left arm forward.
- Every frame the same person: same height, same head size, same coat and patches, same tear on the same knee, same brown boots. Nothing appears or disappears between frames.

STRICT LAYOUT (this is what makes it usable)
- Canvas 2048 x 512, transparent background (real alpha, no white, no checkerboard, no glow, no halo).
- 8 equal cells, each 256 wide x 512 tall. One frame per cell, centred in its cell.
- The soles of the feet on the same line in every cell: 24 pixels above the bottom edge. The foot carrying the weight sits exactly on that line.
- The same scale in every cell: the head top about 40 pixels below the cell top in the "up" frames.
- No ground line, no shadow, no motion blur, no speed lines, no numbers, no grid lines, no text.
- Same ink line weight and colours in all frames. Light from the same side in all frames.

DELIVERY
- incoming-art/anim_pilot/walk_body03_v1.png (and v2, v3 if you try again).
- incoming-art/anim_pilot/walk_body03_prompt.txt with the exact prompt used.
If the first sheet drifts (clothes change, legs do not swap), make a second attempt using the first sheet as a reference and say "keep frame 1 exactly, redraw the others to match it". Send what you have after three attempts; do not polish further.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT (the lead does this, not ChatGPT)
The lead cuts the cells, lines the feet up to one floor, cleans the edges, plays the loop at 10 to 12 frames a second in a real room of the game and records a short clip for the owner. Accept or reject is the owner's call on that clip.
