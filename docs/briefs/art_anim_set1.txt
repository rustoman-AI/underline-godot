Walk first, because it is the proven one.

WALK (8 frames): exactly as the accepted body_03 sheet. True side view facing right, empty hands, arms swing
against the legs. Frames in this order: 1 right heel contact, 2 down on the right leg, 3 left leg passing,
4 up off the right toe, 5 left heel contact, 6 down on the left leg, 7 right leg passing, 8 up off the left toe.
A calm, tired walk.

STAND (4 frames): standing still at rest, side view facing right, weight on both feet, arms down or hands in
coat pockets as fits the person. The frames are one slow breath: 1 neutral, 2 chest and shoulders up very
slightly, 3 neutral, 4 shoulders down very slightly with the head dipping a hair. The difference between frames
is tiny: a few pixels. Feet do not move at all.

WORK (6 frames): side view facing right, working with both hands at waist height on something just outside the
frame on the right (a bench, a valve, a plant rack): the tool or object itself is not drawn. One loop of a steady
task: 1 hands at rest on the work, 2 reach forward, 3 press or turn, 4 hold, 5 draw back, 6 hands at rest again,
matching frame 1 so it loops. Feet planted, they do not move; only arms, shoulders and head move. body_01 may hold
a small spanner from her belt in frames 2 to 5, nobody else holds anything.

STRICT LAYOUT (same as the pilot, this is what makes it usable)
- Transparent background, real alpha, no white, no checkerboard, no glow, no halo.
- One row, equal cells, 256 wide x 512 tall: walk 2048 x 512, stand 1024 x 512, work 1536 x 512.
- Figures do not touch or overlap the neighbouring cell; leave clear space between them.
- Soles on one line in every cell, 24 px above the bottom edge. Same scale in every cell and across all
  sheets of one person: the top of the head about 40 px below the cell top.
- No ground line, no shadow, no blur, no speed lines, no numbers, no grid lines, no text.
- Same ink line weight, colours and light direction as the accepted body_03 walk.
