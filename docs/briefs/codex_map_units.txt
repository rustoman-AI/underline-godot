CODEX ASSIGNMENT: PROFESSIONAL MAP UNITS (art pipeline, animation, motion design)

THE GAME
Underline: a turn-based survival strategy game about one New York subway station after the surface went silent.
Godot 4.7.2, GDScript, Windows. Repo root: C:\Users\V\Desktop\Underline_Godot (git, branch master, local only:
never push). Two screens: the station cross-section (ui/station.gd, yard drawing inside it) and the network map of
stations and tunnels (ui/network_view.gd). Under review for Steam and Epic, so everything shipped must look like a
finished commercial game.

THE PROBLEM
The network map shows units moving between stations, but they are drawn with code primitives (lines, circles,
rectangles) and look like a placeholder: tiny handcars with stick crews, flat discs for squads, a floating icon
bubble over each cart. The owner wants painted art in real animation frames, moving with proper motion design.

WHAT MOVES ON THE MAP TODAY (keep every rule, change only how it looks and moves)
- ui/network_view.gd, layer _unit_layer, function _draw_units(): for every route in _caravans (built by
  _caravan_routes(): kinds "supply" home to the capital, "settle" out to a station still settling in, "trade" out
  and back to deal partners, "rival" from rival stations to their seat), _draw_handcar() draws a handcar along the route.
  Routes are station id chains; screen points come from _layout[sid].at. Helpers: _along(), _poly_length(),
  _route_dots().
- _draw_handcar(): the current code-drawn handcar. _draw_squad_token(): squads on station pins (from
  _draw_squads() in the ink layer) and the expedition party (_draw_expedition()). Squad walks between stations
  use _walks (filled by _track_hops(), eased over WALK_PER_HOP per tunnel).
- Owner colour: _faction_color(game, fid). Trade units use TRADE_GOLD. Load icon: ArtPack.icon_texture(good, px).
- Motion is off when _motion_off() (Settings reduced motion, or screenshot mode without motion_in_shots): units
  stand still at fixed points along their route. Keep that.
- Station view for reference of how frame animation is done in this project: ui/station.gd, ANIM_DIR, ANIM_KINDS,
  _anim(), _anim_frame(), _draw_anim() (frames as assets/figures/anim/<kind>_<body>/f<N>.png, played at a fixed fps,
  offset per person, cut from ChatGPT sheets in incoming-art/anim_set1 and anim_set2).

THE ART
Painted sprite sheets are ordered from ChatGPT with docs/briefs/art_map_tokens.txt (version 2): handcar_pump (8),
handcar_idle (4), trader_car (8), squad_walk (8), squad_stand (4), expedition_walk (8), puff (6). Cells 384 x 384,
transparent, units facing right, rails or soles 48 px above the cell bottom, the owner-colour parts painted exactly
#C8C8C8. They arrive in incoming-art/map_units/ with a HANDOFF.txt. If they are not there yet, build everything
below against simple placeholder sheets you make from the current code drawing (render it into frames), so the art
drops in by replacing files and nothing else.

TASKS
1. Asset pipeline. A small tool script (tools/cut_map_units.gd, run headless) that cuts each sheet into frames,
   trims to a shared bounding box per sheet so the rails or soles stay on one line, and writes
   assets/map/units/<sheet>/f<N>.png. Report frames per sheet and any frame whose content touches a cell edge.
   Never write into assets/ from incoming-art without this tool.
2. Owner tint. A canvas_item shader (ui/shaders/owner_tint.gdshader) that replaces pixels near #C8C8C8 with the
   owner colour while keeping their shading (multiply by the grey's luminance relative to 0.784), leaves every other
   pixel alone, and respects alpha. One material per owner colour, cached.
3. Unit renderer. Replace _draw_handcar and the squad and expedition tokens with sprite drawing from the frames:
   - Scale: units 44 px tall at fit zoom, growing with zoom to at most 76 px; below 30 px switch to a small owner
     disc with the load icon (level of detail), never a smeared sprite.
   - Frame timing tied to distance travelled, so wheels and feet never slide: one pump or walk cycle per
     1.1 x unit width of travel. Idle sheets play at 4 fps when a unit waits.
   - Facing: flip when the unit moves left; smooth the turn over 0.15 s at a route corner instead of popping.
   - Tilt to follow the track, at most 10 degrees, smoothed; people never tilt more than 4 degrees.
   - Soft elliptical shadow under every unit (a small pre-blurred texture), darker when zoomed in.
   - Load: a small paper tag with the good's icon tied to the cart (part of the cart, swinging gently), not a
     bubble floating above it. Trader cars show the deal's goods on the way out and tokens on the way back.
4. Motion design (the part that makes it feel professional):
   - Units ease out of a station (0.4 s) and ease into the next (0.5 s); constant speed between.
   - At each end of a supply or trade route the unit stops for 1.2 s, plays its idle sheet, and a puff plays when
     it starts again. Supply cars unload at the capital (the tag drops off with a tiny bounce), settle cars unload at
     the outpost.
   - Spawn and despawn by fading in or out over 0.25 s at a station, never appearing mid-track.
   - Spacing: units on the same route keep at least 1.6 unit widths apart; on tunnels shared by several routes,
     each owner keeps its own lane offset to the side of the line (supply on the line, trade and rival 10 px to
     either side at fit zoom, scaling with zoom).
   - Squads walking between stations use squad_walk along the real tunnel path (the existing _walks data), then
     squad_stand on the pin. The expedition uses expedition_walk out and back along its tunnel.
   - Puffs behind wheels every 0.6 s while moving fast, none while easing.
5. Performance: only _unit_layer redraws every frame; target under 1 ms per frame with 40 units on a mid laptop.
   Load textures once (no load() in draw calls).
6. Reduced motion and screenshots: with _motion_off() everything stands still at its current fixed points in
   frame 0 (handcar_idle for stopped cars), as today.
7. Verification you must do yourself:
   - Run the screenshot pass: Godot console exe with --path . -- --network (writes shots/network_*.png, including
     network_caravans.png, network_handcar.png, network_squad_walk.png). Look at the images, crop and enlarge the
     unit areas, and compare with the brief.
   - Record a 10-second clip of the map with units moving using Godot's movie maker (--write-movie
     build/clips/map_units.avi --fixed-fps 30 on a scene or flag that opens the network view at week 20 with
     motion on) for the owner.
   - Run all gates: powershell -File .tools/gates.ps1 (9 gates, all must pass). sim/check_clarity.gd already has
     215 known failures about upgrade percentages; do not add new ones (compare the count before and after).
