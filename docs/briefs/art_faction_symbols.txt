ART: FIVE FACTION SYMBOLS (V4)
Leader portraits are DECIDED: the dark set incoming-art/factions_dark/*.png (serious, no smiles). Do not redraw or retouch those faces. This job is the five faction symbols only. Read docs/LEADERS.md for the five factions.

Rejected, do not look at, copy or "improve": incoming-art/rejected/, incoming-art/factions_v3, incoming-art/faction_identities, incoming-art/factions (sepia set). Antigravity does not touch these assets. The project lead reviews before anything is installed.

STYLE
One flat, readable shape plus one accent colour, no text, no gradients, hand-cut and slightly worn, in the same world as assets/hubs/*.webp and the dark portraits (muted, lamp-lit). Readable at 24 px. 1254x1254 transparent PNG.

SYMBOLS
- Depot (rust orange): a track switch lever forming a "Y" fork, seen from above.
- Iron Directorate (brass on dark slate, NOT red): a punched ticket stub with a clock-hand notch.
- Synod of the Tunnel (washed blue): a small lit lamp under a horseshoe tunnel arch. No cross, no ring halo.
- Free Exchange (verdigris and brass): five tally strokes with a diagonal cut, a tiny balance scale as a secondary mark.
- Lamplighters (soot grey and amber): a lantern hanging on a hook, light swaying. NOT a flag or banner.
For reference only, the earlier dark-set symbols (wheel, gauge, flame ring, scales, pennant) are acceptable as generic placeholders but too anonymous; do better. The Lamplighters must not be a flag.

FORBIDDEN
Red field with a white disc, red-white-black triad, hooked or rotated cross arms, runes, eagles, lightning bolts, "black sun", SS forms, skulls, anything resembling a real political badge.

DELIVERY
incoming-art/symbols_v4/<faction>_symbol.png with the exact prompt next to each file, plus a contact sheet: five symbols at 24 px, 64 px and 256 px on the card background colour, and next to the faction colour plate. Do not install into assets/. When these are accepted, the second job is docs/briefs/art_hubs_no_emblems.txt.
