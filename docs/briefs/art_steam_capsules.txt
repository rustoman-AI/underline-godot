ART: STEAM KEY ART AND CAPSULES (ChatGPT image generation)
Draft v1, 17 Oct 2026. Stage 4 of docs/RELEASE_PLAN.md, item 3. Pilot first, then STOP (see ORDER OF WORK).

CONTEXT
Underline is a turn-based strategy game about holding one New York subway station after the Silence: one night the surface stopped answering, and whoever went up never came back. The Steam store needs key art in several fixed sizes. You draw the picture only. There is NO TEXT in anything you deliver: the UNDERLINE logo is made separately and we place it ourselves. Your job is to leave calm, empty room for it in the places given below.

Key line of the game (for mood only, never drawn as text): "Hold one station. Keep the lights on. Do not ask what happened up there."

STYLE (locked, do not change)
Open these before drawing and match them:
- incoming-art/hubs/style_v2/brickworks.png (the target drawing)
- incoming-art/hubs/coney.png (ignore the station sign in its top left; never copy any lettering)
The look: one consistent ink outline weight, flat cel colour with at most one shadow tone per surface, muted blue grey and ochre base, very light paper grain. Large calm planes of wall, floor and ceiling. Not a render: no micro detail everywhere, no bloom, no glow haze, no lens flare, no god rays, no glossy wet floors, no mirror reflections, no rows of lamps, no cinematic "epic" lighting.
The key art must look like it belongs to the same game as the station pictures. If a draft looks more impressive than brickworks.png but in a different style, it is wrong.

THE PICTURE: "THE LAST LAMP"
Place: the bottom of a station exit stairway at the end of the Coney line. A long, steep, plain concrete stair climbs out of frame into flat darkness. At the very top, barely visible, a closed folding steel gate, pulled shut. Nothing comes through it: no light, no sky, no glow, no colour. Above is simply dark.
Main subject: at the foot of the stairs, one tired person (the keeper; any gender, about 50, patched work coat, practical) is reaching up to hang or adjust a single caged work lamp on a riveted column. That lamp is the only real light in the picture. Its warm ochre light makes one flat pool on the floor and catches the keeper's sleeve and face from below.
Second beat: a little apart, a child (about 9, oversized coat) stands on the bottom step looking UP the stairs into the dark. The keeper does not look up. That contrast is the whole story of the picture.
Background: two or three adults on the platform behind, simple silhouettes or small shapes with no faces drawn: someone carrying a water can, someone sitting on a bedroll. A plain cot, a crate, a coil of cable. Platform edge and a dark tunnel or the side of a dead train car toward one side, kept very quiet.
Mood: tired, careful, quiet. People keep going. Nobody is heroic.

RULES
1. One main subject (the keeper and the lamp), readable in one second, off centre.
2. At most five people in total. Nobody smiles. Nobody looks at the viewer. No posing, no weapons raised, no gas masks, no hazmat suits.
3. Exactly one dominant lamp. At most one more faint light source (a candle stub far back) if the composition needs it.
4. The top of the stairs stays dark and plain. Do NOT show the sky, the city, ruins, fire, an aurora, a bright flash, radiation signs, a glow, a light beam coming down, bodies, or anything that explains what happened up there. The picture must not answer that question.
5. No text, letters, numbers, signs with glyphs, station name tiles, arrows, posters, flags, insignia, emblems or logos anywhere. Wall tiles are plain. Paper is grey scribble.
6. No faction colours as flags or banners. Clothing is plain and mended.
7. Floors matte, a small dull puddle at most.
8. Large calm planes, especially in the LOGO ZONES below: dark wall or dark stairwell, low contrast, no figures, no lamp, no detail that would fight with lettering.

RESOLUTION
Image models do not produce 3840 pixels natively. Generate at the largest size you can at the right aspect and report the actual size. Do NOT use an AI upscaler that invents detail: added micro detail is exactly the "AI look" we removed from the hubs. If the master is smaller than the target, the project lead scales it with a plain resampler, which keeps flat cel colour clean.

ORDER OF WORK (pilot, then stop)
STAGE 1, PILOT (one image)
- Master A only, one take. Exact prompt saved next to it.
- Plus one contact sheet: the master with the crop rectangles of sizes 1, 2, 3 and 5 drawn on it as thin grey boxes, and each logo safe area shaded at 30% grey. No lettering on the sheet except the file name in the sheet's margin if needed.
- Plus the master placed side by side with incoming-art/hubs/style_v2/brickworks.png at the same height.
- STOP. Do not draw Master B or any variant. The project lead and the owner review.

STAGE 2, AFTER "go" FOR STAGE 2
- Master B, one take, same deliverables (crop boxes for sizes 4 and 8, logo zones shaded). STOP again.

STAGE 3, AFTER "go" FOR STAGE 3
- Only the fixes asked for in review, at most two more takes per master. Then final PNG masters. We crop and export all Steam sizes ourselves.

STOP AND REPORT AT ONCE IF
- any letters, numbers or glyph-like marks appear and cannot be removed in the same take;
- the style drifts toward a render (bloom, glow, glossy floor, micro detail) twice in a row;
- the top of the stairs shows light, sky or anything that explains the Silence;
- a face smiles or looks at the viewer;
- you cannot keep a logo zone calm without breaking the composition. Then propose a different layout in words before drawing it.
Never deliver more than one take per stage without asking. No batches of variants.
