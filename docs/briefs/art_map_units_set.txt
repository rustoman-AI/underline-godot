ART: MAP UNITS SET (ChatGPT image tool) — only after the handcar pilot is accepted

WHERE WE ARE
The handcar pilot (docs/briefs/art_map_units_pilot.txt) gave an accepted handcar reference and pump loop. This set
draws the other units that move on the network map, the same way: for each unit first one still reference (the
owner accepts it), then its loops with that reference attached to every attempt. Match the accepted handcar
exactly in style, scale, line weight and light. Attach to every generation: the accepted handcar_reference and
handcar_pump sheets, plus the references named below.

SAME RULES AS THE PILOT
- TRUE SIDE VIEW, facing right. Ink outline, flat colour with a little texture, muted palette, no gloss.
- The owner-colour part painted PLAIN LIGHT GREY #C8C8C8 and nothing else uses that grey.
- Transparent background, real alpha, no glow, no halo, no ground, no shadow, no text, no motion blur.
- A WIDE EMPTY GAP between frames (at least a quarter of a frame's width); same scale in every frame; the rails or
  the soles on one straight line in every frame.
- Every frame the same people and objects: nothing appears, disappears or changes colour.
- At most three attempts per sheet; "keep frame 1 exactly, redraw the others to match it" for the second attempt.

THE UNITS (in this order; each is a reference, then its sheets)
1. HANDCAR IDLE (no new reference, use the accepted handcar)
   handcar_idle: 4 frames, the car standing still at a station. Front worker wipes his brow with a sleeve (frames
   2 and 3), back worker leans on her end of the lever, both breathe. Lever level throughout.
2. TRADER CAR
   trader_reference: a longer four-wheeled handcar with a canvas canopy on hoops over crates and bundles; the canopy
   is the grey part. A hanging lantern at the front. One trader in a long patched coat and scarf works a side pump
   handle. Like the handcar in materials and size of people.
   trader_roll: 6 frames, the trader pumping (handle down, lowest, up, level, as in the pilot), wheels turning one
   sixth of a turn a frame, the lantern swinging a little forward and back.
3. SQUAD (three people on foot, no vehicle)
   squad_reference: three guards side by side in step, gas masks pushed up on their heads, padded coats, grey
   armbands (the grey part). Front one holds a lamp up, middle one a long pipe-spear on the shoulder, back one a
   round scrap-metal shield. Faces like the station residents (attach reference_body_07.png and
   reference_body_11.png from incoming-art/anim_pilot/ for two of them).
   squad_walk: 6 frames, one calm march cycle (two steps): contact, down, passing, contact (other leg), down,
   passing. All three in step. The lamp, spear and shield keep their size and shape.
   squad_stand: 4 frames, standing guard, small shift of weight, the lamp flame flickering.
4. EXPEDITION (two people on foot)
   expedition_reference: two scouts with big packs and coiled rope; the front one carries a pole with a small grey
   pennant (the grey part) and a lamp, the back one a pick over the shoulder.
   expedition_walk: 6 frames, a heavy, slow walking cycle, the same leg order as the squad.
5. DUST PUFF
   puff: 6 frames of a small grey-brown dust puff from appearing to gone, side view, no unit.

DELIVERY
incoming-art/map_units/<name>_v1.png (v2, v3) and <name>_prompt.txt for every reference and sheet, plus HANDOFF.txt
as in the pilot (preferred versions, real canvas sizes, frames near a gap, anything that changes between frames).
Do not install anything into assets/.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT
The lead finds the frames by the gaps, aligns them on the rail or sole line and cuts them into assets/map/units/
with tools/cut_map_units.gd; Codex plays them on the map (docs/briefs/codex_map_units.txt). The owner accepts on a
clip of the map.
