2. The upgrade is shown by OBJECTS, not by effects: new equipment, repairs, better materials, extra furniture. No glow clouds, no floating icons, no arrows, no text, no labels, no numbers.
3. Tier 1 is a modest, handmade improvement. Tier 2 is clearly more advanced AND still contains everything of tier 1 (so the three images read as a progression: source, tier 1, tier 2).
4. Post-apocalypse look: scrap, welded plates, salvaged parts, cable, tape. Nothing clean or new. Nobody smiles; if a person is in the source, keep them as is. Do not add new people or faces. No real symbols, no flags, no party signs.
5. Transparent background where the source has transparency; same alpha edges.
6. Output: PNG at the source size (the lead converts to webp). One image per upgrade.
