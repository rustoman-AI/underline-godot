ART: FIVE PORTRAITS FOR THE CONEY CAST
Why: in every game the five residents chris, mina, kim, drew, ann are renamed to Old Man Coney, Salty Maggie, Bait, Barnum and Sylvia the Mermaid (_apply_coney_cast), but they keep a random portrait chosen by hash from assets/portraits/portrait_01..16. Bait shows a woman, Sylvia shows an old man, Old Man Coney shows an old woman. Draw one portrait for each cast member. Antigravity does not touch these assets. Nothing is installed into assets/ until the project lead accepts it.

STYLE (match the existing resident portraits, look at assets/portraits/portrait_03.webp and a few others)
Aged sepia photograph with a worn paper border, a station tunnel behind the person (tiles, pipes, a lamp), the person facing the camera in a bust, 1:1 square, 1024x1024 source (the game uses 512). Serious or tired. NOBODY SMILES. Real human faces with age and wear, not caricature; the costume is the character. No text, no logos, no real people. Same sepia tone and grain across the five so they sit together in the People list.

THE FIVE (look text comes from data/characters_coney.json, use it exactly)
1. old_man_coney, Old Man Coney, Keeper of the Rusted Rides. Frail but wiry old man. He wears a balding plush tiger head from an old amusement ride in place of a helmet, a cigarette poking out of the tiger's mouth, torn pea coat. His weathered face is visible under the tiger's chin. Gone strange from years alone by the ocean.
2. salty_maggie, Salty Maggie, Queen of the Salt Pans. Heavyset woman of about fifty, skin crusted with salt, an apron cut from a subway car floor mat, a huge ladle always in hand. Hard and cynical.
3. bait, Bait, Harpooner of the Dead Pier. Thin man, ONE-EYED, tattooed with gulls and anchors, his left arm below the elbow is a homemade spring harpoon. Speaks in a whisper, keeps checking the tension of a line.
4. barnum, Barnum, Keeper of the Curiosity Cabinet. Small and stooped man in a yellowed lab coat over a wool sweater. Thick taped-up glasses make his eyes enormous. Former taxidermist or maybe museum thief. A glass jar on a shelf behind him is fine; no gore.
5. sylvia, Sylvia the Mermaid, Voice of the Surf. Young woman wrapped in torn fishing nets and plastic seaweed, face painted blue, a pre-war megaphone on a rope round her neck. Preacher of the Great Tide. Not smiling: a calm, certain face.
