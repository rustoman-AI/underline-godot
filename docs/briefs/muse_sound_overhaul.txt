STEP 3: STYLE RULES (what "professional" means here)
 Real materials, not synth beeps: paper, brass, wood, rusty metal, stamp on a ledger, radio static, a pencil mark, a switch, a heavy door. Short, soft, low in level. No 8-bit, no chip tones, no rising "reward" arpeggios. End turn: a calm, low, heavy sound (a ledger closing, a lever, a deep thud with a short room tail), not a bright jingle. Dossier: paper and a clip, a page turn and a small stamp, quiet. Loading and menu: a low room tone with distant ticks, no loop that grates over 30 seconds. Nothing louder than the voice-over. Pitch variation: 3 variants for the click and card sounds so repetition does not tire.
