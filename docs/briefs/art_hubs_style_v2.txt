ART: HUBS, STYLE PASS (less "AI", same places)
Context: the four faction hubs (northgate, chapel, trampark, brickworks) have the right places and the right moods, and the emblem-free versions in incoming-art/hubs/no_emblems_v1 are technically accepted. The project lead's verdict: the art is liked but looks too AI-made. Do not install no_emblems_v1; redo the four hubs in the style below, still emblem-free.

WHY THEY LOOK AI-MADE
Every square inch has the same level of micro-detail. Dozens of near-identical figures. A lantern glow on everything. Soft bloom, glossy wet floor reflections. Perfectly symmetric halls. No empty space for the eye, no single subject. It reads as a render, not a drawing. Also, it does not match Coney (incoming-art/hubs/coney.png): flat cel colour, one clear line weight, simple shapes, a few warm light sources, large calm planes.

TARGET
Match the Coney hub: ink outlines with one consistent weight, flat or two-tone shading, muted palette, paper grain, light only from two or three clear sources (one dominant). Wide panorama, same aspect ratio as now.

RULES
1. One subject per hub, readable in one second (north: the information booth and the closed gate; chapel: the kitchen line under the chained exit; trampark: the weighing table and the stack of sacks; brickworks: the guide with a lantern at a tunnel mouth). Everything else is quieter and simpler.
2. At most 7 readable figures per hub. Background people are silhouettes or shapes, not faces. No repeated figure, no crowd wall.
3. At most 3 visible lamps per hub, each doing a job. No lantern strings, no bloom, no lens glow.
4. Large calm areas: wall, floor, ceiling shapes left plain with just texture and a stain or two. Detail is spent on 3 to 5 story props (a ration card, a hand-painted queue sign without letters, a mended coat, a child's boot).
5. Break the symmetry. Off-centre the subject, crop a figure at the frame edge, let one thing be wrong or unfinished.
6. Hands and faces simple but human: no perfect pores or glossy eyes. Tired, practical, not posing. No one smiles. No staring at the viewer.
7. Floors: matte stone, a puddle at most. No mirror reflections.
8. Still emblem-free: banners and patches are plain cloth. Directorate cloth is dark slate or green, no red field, no white disc. Lamplighters: no flags at all.
9. No text, letters, signs with glyphs, numbers.
