# Underline: closed playtest (version 0.1.0-playtest1)

Thank you for playing. The game is early: a few rough edges are normal. What matters most to us is your honest experience.

## What it is
A strategy game about one New York subway station after the Silence. You hold a station on the Coney line, dig, build, staff rooms, pass laws and talk to the leaders of five powers. One run is 40 weeks, about 2 to 3 hours.

## Install (Windows)
1. Unzip to any folder (do not run it from inside the archive).
2. Run `Underline.exe`. If Windows warns that it protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway. The build is unsigned and contains nothing extra.
3. The language follows your system and can be changed in Settings.

## What to do
- Play one full run (or at least 10 weeks if you are short on time). Play the way you normally would; do not optimise.
- If you want a second run, play it differently (other choices, other leaders).
- There are no guides to look up. Keep notes as you go if you like.

## What we record
The game writes a local log: version, language, resolution, time per week, your decisions and the ending. **Nothing is sent automatically.** There is no name, email, network access or personal data in the log. The Menu has Open log folder and Copy report. After playing, send us the log file (or the copied report) with the survey.

## How to send it
1. Menu, Copy report, paste it into your reply.
2. Fill in the survey (link in the message, 5 to 8 minutes).
3. If the game crashed or froze, send the newest file from Open log folder.

## Good to know
- Mouse is enough. Wheel or pinch zooms the station.
- It autosaves every week. Continue returns you to the last week.
- Known rough edges: no logo or intro, some events are still being written, some leader lines are voiced and some are silent.
- Please do not post screenshots or video without asking us first while the closed test is running.

Thank you!
